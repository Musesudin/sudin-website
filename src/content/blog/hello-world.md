I finally did it: a site of my own, on my own domain. No algorithm deciding who sees what, no character limits, just a place to write.

## What I'll write about

Three things take up most of my headspace, so that's what you'll find here:

- **Fitness**: how I train, what I eat, what has worked and what hasn't.
- **Tech**: the things I build, the tools I rely on, and what I'm learning.
- **Everything else**: books, ideas, and anything I can't stop talking about.

## How to follow along

The easiest way is [RSS](/rss.xml): paste that link into any feed reader and new posts will show up automatically. You can also find me on the [contact page](/contact/).
